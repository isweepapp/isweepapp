👑 Champion
All Hail Martin!

After 48 group stage matches across 12 groups, Martin (Ludus Tantum Est) has swept to the Group Stage crown with an impressive 64 points — a full 6 clear of the chasing pack. Backed by England, Japan, Switzerland, Cape Verde, DR Congo and Jordan, it was a masterclass in pot selection. Take a bow. 🥇

The battle for silver was settled on the tiebreaker. Both John (Saka Can Saka Can) and Twiglet (I spy FC) finished locked on 58 points, sending it to the goals prediction — closest to the projected 309 tournament goals (based on 2.97 goals/game across 104 matches). John predicted 204 (just 105 away) to Twiglet's 155 (154 away), handing John the silver medal by the finest of margins. 🥈🥉

Honourable mentions to Gavin (FCTW) in 4th with 52 points, Drakey hot on his heels in 5th with 50, and Licence Tuchel (Scum) sneaking into 6th with 49 points despite an audacious goals prediction of 412 — bold, very bold. 😄
